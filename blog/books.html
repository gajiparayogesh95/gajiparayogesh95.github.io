Where to Start

If you are new to this and want a sequence rather than a list: read The Intelligent Investor for the philosophy, then Competition Demystified to understand why some businesses earn better returns than others, then The Outsiders to see what good capital allocation looks like in practice. After those three, follow your curiosity.
